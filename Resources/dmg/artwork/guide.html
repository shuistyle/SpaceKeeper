First time opening SpaceKeeper

macOS checks apps from the internet the first time you open them. SpaceKeeper isn't sold through Apple, so you need to tell your Mac once that it's OK. This takes about a minute and you only do it once.

  1. Copy SpaceKeeper into Applications

    In the SpaceKeeper window, drag the SpaceKeeper icon onto the Applications folder. (Skip this if you've already done it.)

  2. Try to open it

    Open Finder, click Applications in the sidebar, then double-click SpaceKeeper. Your Mac shows a message like this:

    What you'll see
    “SpaceKeeper” Not OpenedApple could not verify “SpaceKeeper” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.
    Move to BinDone

    Click Done. Don't click Move to Bin (it may say Move to Trash).

  3. Open Privacy & Security

    Click the Apple menu (the apple logo) in the top-left corner of the screen, choose System Settings, then click Privacy & Security in the sidebar.

  4. Click “Open Anyway”

    Scroll down to the Security section. You'll see this:

    What you'll see
    “SpaceKeeper” was blocked to protect your Mac.Open Anyway

    Click Open Anyway, then type your Mac password (or use Touch ID). If your Mac asks once more, click Open Anyway again.

    Can't see Open Anyway? It only shows for about an hour after step 2. Double-click SpaceKeeper in Applications again, click Done, and look again.

  5. SpaceKeeper is open

    SpaceKeeper has no window of its own. It lives in the menu bar at the top-right of your screen as a small icon of stacked squares. Click it to open the panel. From now on it opens normally, like any other app.

    Can't see the icon? Open System Settings › Menu Bar and make sure SpaceKeeper is switched on.

Getting started

  1. Keep your desktops in order

    In the SpaceKeeper panel, turn on Keep Spaces in a fixed order. The Dock restarts once; that's normal.

  2. Allow switching

    Click Enable Switching…. In Privacy & Security › Accessibility, switch SpaceKeeper on. This lets SpaceKeeper move you between desktops. If the panel shows Turn On Shortcuts, click it too.

  3. Name, colour and pin

    Double-click a desktop to give it a name. Right-click it to choose a colour or pin it in place. Click a desktop to go to it.

  4. Open SpaceKeeper quickly

    Press the control key twice quickly, or click the menu bar icon.

Is this safe?

The warning appears because SpaceKeeper isn't registered with Apple's paid developer programme. It doesn't mean anything is wrong with it. SpaceKeeper is free and open source: anyone can read its code on GitHub. It doesn't collect or send any of your information.

Only download SpaceKeeper from its official GitHub page. If you got it from somewhere else, delete it.

Removing SpaceKeeper

Open the SpaceKeeper panel and choose Quit. Then drag SpaceKeeper from Applications to the Bin.

When you've finished installing

You can close the SpaceKeeper install window and eject the SpaceKeeper disk in Finder's sidebar (click ⏏ next to it). You can delete the downloaded .dmg file too.
